Entry for the make.fun bounty **Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line**:
one sentence, up to 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in
everyday life, judged pairwise by an AI judge that rewards surprise.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network, no randomness, no
timestamps) writes a single hard-coded sentence to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My neighbour waters the same dead plant every morning, and today a green shoot answered him back.

The sentence was picked from three original drafts against the judge's
brief: a small everyday moment, an angle the reader has not met before
(the plant answering back), the surprise landing in one read, grounded in
something real and specific, with every word working.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output.
